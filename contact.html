Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you with your booking or cake order.

How far in advance should I order?

For custom wedding cakes, we recommend 3-6 months. For standard birthday cakes, 2 weeks is usually sufficient.

Are materials included in workshops?

Yes! All ingredients, tools, and aprons are provided. Just bring yourself and a container to take your cake home.

Do you offer vegan options?

Absolutely. We offer a full range of vegan, gluten-free, and dairy-free options for both orders and specific workshops.

What is your cancellation policy?

Workshop cancellations require 72 hours notice for a full refund. Custom cake deposits are non-refundable.
